# v0.3.1 — Out of your way.

Starting Sushi no longer puts the panel in front of you: after a login or `brew upgrade` you get the menu bar icon and the key, and the panel waits for **Caps Lock**. **Report a problem…** opens in a window of its own that stays where you put it, and a sign-in that got stuck has a way out.

## Starts quietly

- Opening Sushi, at login or when Homebrew opens it again after an upgrade, shows the menu bar icon and nothing else. **Caps Lock** shows the panel, and so does opening Sushi again while it runs.
- The panel still shows by itself the first time Sushi starts on a Mac.

## A report in a window of its own

- **Report a problem…**, in the menu bar menu and in Settings ▸ About, opens a normal window. It stays open while you look something up in another app, moves and resizes, and shows in Mission Control. In v0.3.0 the report lived in the panel and went out of sight at every click elsewhere.
- The panel works as usual next to it, so you can try the problem again while the report waits. During a recording the window stays at *Recording…*; finish it there, at the top of the panel or in the menu bar menu.
- **⌘↵** copies the report and closes the window, so you can paste at once; **⌘S** saves it and shows the folder in Finder. The report stays as you left it until you choose **Done**.
- ⌘↵ no longer stops working after a click on **Save…** or on the checkbox of the recording.

[Report a problem →](/guide/report/)

## Connecting a provider

- While Sushi waits for your browser to sign in to OpenRouter, **Cancel** next to *Connecting…* stops the wait. Before, a closed tab left Settings waiting for five minutes.
- The sheet that takes an OpenAI or Claude key says under the field what you pasted: the key's first and last characters, the ones the provider's keys page shows, and its length. A long key used to leave a few dots in view, which looked like a wrong paste.

[The model →](/guide/model/)

## Fixed

- With a window of macOS over the panel, such as the **Summary** of Writing Tools, Caps Lock hides the panel and shows it again with that window. Before, the second Caps Lock made the panel flicker and stay hidden.
- **Copy build info** in Settings ▸ About and `sushi version` say the day the release was made. v0.3.0 said `2023-06-17`.
- A long library path in Settings ▸ Library wraps after a slash, not inside a folder's name.

## Good to know

- **Checked with real accounts.** OpenRouter, OpenAI and Claude were connected and used for edits with real accounts for this release; v0.3.0 had run against a test server only.
- **OpenRouter says *Unauthorized request* after you sign in.** Sign in at openrouter.ai first, in the same browser, then click **Connect…** again. [Troubleshooting →](/guide/troubleshooting/#connecting-openrouter-does-not-finish)

**Upgrade:** run `brew upgrade --cask octolab/tap/sushi`. Homebrew quits Sushi, replaces it and opens it again, now with the panel closed.
