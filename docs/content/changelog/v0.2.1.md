# v0.2.1 — Stays where you work.

Press **Caps Lock** over a full-screen app and the panel comes up on top of it, the way Spotlight does: no slide to another Space, and the app underneath keeps its menus. A click anywhere else puts the panel away, and every edit now says what it did, even when it did nothing.

Thanks to @dmigor for the bug reports behind this release.

## Over any app, without moving you

- The panel shows on the Space you are in, over a full-screen app too. That app stays in full screen, and focus goes back to it when the panel hides.
- The panel no longer appears in Mission Control or App Exposé.
- A click in another app or on the desktop hides the panel, as it hides Spotlight, and focus stays where you clicked. Your draft is still there the next time you press **Caps Lock**.

## A right click is a right click

- A right click in the editor opens the usual macOS menu, with Look Up, Translate, spelling and Services, whether or not text is selected. Actions stay on **⌘K** and the **Action** button.

## What an edit did

- When the model returns your text unchanged, the line above the bar says *No changes: the model returned the text as it was* and stays until your next step, so you can tell it from an edit still running. Try again in other words: a small model may leave a change of meaning alone, so name the change exactly, such as *replace X with Y*.
- After an edit, the same line names the model, how long it took and how many places changed: **Shorten · qwen3.5:4b-mlx · 2.6 s · 4 changes**. *1 change* in a long paragraph tells you to look closely.

## Ollama starts in the background

- When an edit starts Ollama, the Ollama app opens with its menu bar icon only. Its window no longer covers your work, and the panel stays open until the edit arrives.

## Good to know

- **Dictating with MacWhisper.** MacWhisper places its recording bubble at the bottom of the text field, and Sushi's field is the whole prompt. With a prompt taller than the panel, the bubble ends up below the screen. Dictation still works and the text lands in the panel.

**Upgrade:** run `brew upgrade --cask octolab/tap/sushi`. Homebrew quits Sushi, replaces it and opens it again.
