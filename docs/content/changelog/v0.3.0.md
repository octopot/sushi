# v0.3.0 — Your model, your settings.

**Settings…** in the menu bar menu, or **⌘,** in the panel, opens a window for everything you used to change in files: the key that shows Sushi, the model, your actions and the library. Edits can now run on OpenRouter, OpenAI or Claude as well as on Ollama, and when something goes wrong, Sushi helps you say what.

Thanks to @gorshkovdn for the report and the patch behind the Writing Tools fix.

## Settings, in a window

![The Keyboard pane of the settings window](/changelog/v0.3.0/settings-keyboard.png)

- Five panes: **Keyboard**, **Model**, **Actions**, **Library** and **About**.
- There is no Save button: a change is saved when you make it. What is not complete yet stays in the window, marked *Not saved*, until it is.
- The files are still yours. Everything is kept where it was, in the same format, and each pane shows its file in Finder. An edit you make by hand shows in the window, and Sushi never overwrites a change it has not seen: when the file changed under your edit, you choose which version to keep.
- A file Sushi cannot read is named in its pane with what is wrong. **Restore defaults** starts over and keeps the broken file next to it.

## The key that shows Sushi

![Recording a keyboard shortcut](/changelog/v0.3.0/settings-keyboard-shortcut.png)

- Choose **Caps Lock** or **Keyboard shortcut**, and record the shortcut by pressing it.
- A shortcut another app already holds is not saved, and the pane says which key still works.
- When Caps Lock is not available, for example because macOS uses it to switch input sources, the pane says why and what to do, and the backup shortcut shows Sushi meanwhile.
- The menu bar menu shows the key in effect next to **Show Sushi**.

## Pick your model

![The Model pane with OpenRouter open](/changelog/v0.3.0/settings-model.png)

- **OpenRouter** connects by a sign-in in your browser: no key to copy. **OpenAI** and **Claude** take an API key that you paste once.
- Keys are kept in the macOS keychain, never in a file.
- Before you connect, Sushi says what leaves your Mac, the instruction and the text you edit, and who pays for it.
- **Model** in the menu bar menu names the model in use and switches between the connected providers and Ollama in one click.
- When a hosted edit fails, the message says why, and **Edit with Ollama** runs the same edit on your Mac.
- Ollama stays the default: until you connect a provider, nothing leaves your Mac.

## Your actions and dictionary

![Editing an action](/changelog/v0.3.0/settings-actions.png)

- Add, duplicate, reorder and delete the **⌘K** actions, with **Undo** after a delete. Each action has its instruction and the phrases that find it, typed or dictated.
- The dictionary of dictated terms is a table next to the library folder.
- A broken dictionary no longer stops your actions, and the other way round.

## A shorter menu

**Show Sushi**, **Model**, **Settings…**, **Report a problem…**, **About Sushi** and **Quit Sushi**. A row about the key appears only when the key you chose does not work, and it opens the pane that says why.

## When something goes wrong

![The About pane](/changelog/v0.3.0/settings-about.png)

- **About** shows the version, and **Copy build info** copies which build you run.
- **Report a problem…** asks what went wrong, in your own words, and up to three questions. It can record while you repeat the steps: when the panel shows and hides and the shortcuts you press, never your text. You get one report to read, edit and send yourself. Sushi never sends it.
- **Log panel activity** in About records why the panel hides, for a problem that comes and goes.

## The panel stays open

- When Writing Tools shows its result over the panel, such as a Summary, the panel no longer hides, and Caps Lock no longer makes it flicker.

## Good to know

- **OpenRouter key from a file.** If you had put an OpenRouter key into `model.json`, Sushi moves it to the keychain the first time it starts.
- **Hosted models are new.** If an edit through OpenRouter, OpenAI or Claude fails in a way the message does not explain, tell us with **Report a problem…**.
- **Susi.** Sushi no longer brings settings over from Susi, its name before v0.2.0. The [quick start](/quick-start/) says how to copy them by hand.

**Upgrade:** run `brew upgrade --cask octolab/tap/sushi`. Homebrew quits Sushi, replaces it and opens it again.
